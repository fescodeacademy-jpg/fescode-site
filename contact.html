FAQ

Questions we often get.

How long does a typical project take?

A marketing website can take 2–4 weeks; a custom web platform or AI product typically takes 2–4 months. We confirm timelines in writing after the discovery call.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. We're remote‑first and have delivered projects for clients in Ethiopia and the USA. We adapt our working hours for smooth collaboration.

Will I own the source code?

Yes. On final payment, you receive full ownership of the source code, documentation and deployment credentials, as set out in our agreement.

How do I apply for SIWES placement?

Use the form above and choose “SIWES placement”. Include your institution, course of study and placement period, and we'll get back to you with next steps.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance plans covering updates, monitoring, bug fixes and small feature additions.
