Our story

Started by an engineer who loves to ship.

FesCode Limited began with a simple belief: great software can be built anywhere, including right here in Nigeria. What started as one engineer taking on ambitious projects grew into a registered company delivering web platforms, AI products and data solutions to clients in Africa and the United States.

Today we combine product engineering with a strong focus on people. Alongside client work, we run practical training programmes and host SIWES students, giving them real projects and real mentorship.

  • CAC‑registered corporate entity based in FCT, Abuja
